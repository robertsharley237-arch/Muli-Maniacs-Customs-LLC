About Multi-Maniacs Customs LLC

Multi-Maniacs Customs LLC is a multi-industry customization and freelance business based in Essie, KY, serving clients across the United States. We specialize in custom builds, custom products, and unique one-off projects tailored to each client’s vision.

Our Mission

To bring wild ideas, bold concepts, and personal visions to life through high-quality custom work, attention to detail, and a relentless commitment to creativity.

Who We Work With

We work with individuals, small businesses, enthusiasts, and anyone who wants something that doesn’t exist off the shelf. If you can imagine it, we’ll help you build it.
