About Multi-Maniacs Customs LLC

Multi-Maniacs Customs LLC is a multi-industry customization and freelance business based in Mozelle, KY, co-owned by Harley and Randy Whitehead.

We are not a body shop. Instead, we focus on custom products, custom builds, and custom jobs across many different industries. Our work is driven by our clients’ ideas, needs, and goals.

We work with clients—not customers. Every project is personal, unique, and built to match the client’s exact vision, whether it’s automotive, home, apparel, tools, protection systems, or other custom projects.
