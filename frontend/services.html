From Idea to Completion

How the Custom Project Process Works

Custom projects begin with information gathering so MMC can understand the request before discussing pricing, materials, production, and scheduling.

1

Submit Your Idea

Share the project type, description, goals, budget, timeline, location, measurements, and available reference images.

2

Project Review

MMC reviews the request and identifies questions, material needs, customization options, and possible project limitations.

3

Scope and Pricing

The project scope, estimated cost, payment requirements, production expectations, and timeline are discussed with the client.

4

Creation and Completion

After approval, MMC begins the project and communicates with the client about progress, changes, and completion arrangements.
