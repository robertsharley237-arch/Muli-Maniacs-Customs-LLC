Our Services

Multi-Maniacs Customs is a multi‑industry customization and freelance business offering a wide range of creative, practical, and professional services. We customize products, upgrade homes, work with pets, and handle hands‑on projects of all kinds.

Product Customization

  • Custom clothing (printing, embroidery, patches, alterations)
  • Custom jewelry (engraving, design, repair)
  • Custom tools and equipment
  • Home interior customization and décor
  • Unique one‑off creations and personal projects

Freelance & Hands‑On Services

  • Demolition work (small structures, interior tear‑outs)
  • Landscaping and yard cleanup
  • Pet grooming and pet care services
  • Installation of home protection systems
  • Security camera setup
  • Pet perimeter systems and containment setups
  • General labor and project assistance

If you need something customized, built, installed, repaired, or designed — MMC has you covered.
